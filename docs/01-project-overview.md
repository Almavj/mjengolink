# Project overview

## What MjengoLink is

MjengoLink is a responsive web application for the Eldoret / Uasin Gishu construction-job pilot. It connects three kinds of people in one workspace:

- **Clients** post construction jobs, review interested fundis, assign work, track progress, and leave job-linked feedback.
- **Fundis** maintain trade profiles, discover suitable kazi, express interest, and participate in client-initiated chats after being accepted.
- **Admins** oversee platform metrics, accounts, reports, and moderation.

The product is not a marketplace with payments or escrow. It is a lightweight matching and coordination layer for a real-world pilot. Trust signals are deliberately modest: ratings describe past job-linked experience, not identity verification, insurance, or trade certification.

## Product language

| Term | Meaning in this codebase |
|---|---|
| Kazi | A job / work opportunity |
| Fundi | A skilled worker / tradesperson |
| Client | The person or organisation posting a job |
| Job | A persisted construction opportunity with trade, location, budget, and start date |
| Interest | A fundi's response to a job |
| Response | The database record representing interest, with status `Interested`, `Accepted`, or `Declined` |
| Match | A fundi eligible for a new-job alert: available, trade matches, and inside the job radius when coordinates exist |
| Conversation | A private chat thread opened by the client after accepting a fundi |
| Notification | An in-app record in the Bell menu; separate from email delivery |

## Core capabilities

### Authentication and onboarding

- Email/password accounts through Supabase Auth.
- Email confirmation and password recovery links generated by Supabase and delivered through Brevo.
- Registration collects the user's own name, location, and intended role — no default location is assigned.
- Fundi registration also collects a primary trade and optional phone number.
- A one-time role choice is presented for older accounts that have no recognised role.
- Admin access is granted manually in SQL, never through the UI.

### Client workspace

- Post and edit jobs while they are still `Posted` or `Interested`.
- See active jobs, work in progress, completed work, platform metrics, fundi directory, and work history.
- Review fundis who showed interest, then accept or decline each response.
- Open a private chat only after accepting a fundi.
- Advance a job through a server-enforced lifecycle.
- Rate the accepted fundi after the job is `Completed`.

### Fundi workspace

- See a kazi board filtered by trade and search text.
- Maintain a persisted profile: trades, service area, phone, experience, summary, availability, and optional coordinates.
- Express interest in an open job, with server-side duplicate and same-day safeguards.
- View own responses and completed work history.
- Toggle availability; only available fundis enter new-job matching.
- Set a profile photo from the account panel.

### Admin workspace

- View real platform metrics derived from database records.
- Inspect accounts and jobs.
- Review and resolve submitted reports.
- See pilot-health indicators such as profile completeness, job location completeness, and rating coverage.

### Notifications and email

- In-app Bell notifications for new jobs, interest, decisions, opened chats, and new messages.
- Toast when the unread count increases.
- Notification list refreshes every 10 seconds.
- Brevo emails for authentication, new-job fan-out, accepted interest, opened chats, and new messages.
- New jobs are never broadcast to all users; matching rules protect fundi attention and client privacy.

## Scope boundaries

The pilot intentionally excludes:

- Payments, escrow, invoicing, and payroll.
- Automated identity-document verification.
- Insurance and formal trade certification.
- Dispute adjudication and contract enforcement.
- Contractor tendering and government employment-database integration.
- Push notifications on mobile devices.
- Notification preference controls and quiet hours.

These are product boundaries, not accidental omissions. Do not add sensitive fields or workflows to the `dashboard.accounts` payload without reviewing the authorization model.

## Design direction

The UI follows the **Kijiji Modernism** direction documented in the historical `plan.md`:

- Mjengo Green `#0B5D43`, terracotta, mustard, warm sand, and charcoal ink.
- A slim Kenyan stripe as an orientation mark.
- Monospace field labels for IDs, locations, dates, and money.
- Dense but practical workbench layouts rather than marketing-style hero pages.
- Short, direct copy: “Post a job”, “Show interest”, “Give job”, “Open chat”.
- Feedback through compact toasts, not blocking success pages.
